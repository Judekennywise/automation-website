Every term, the same pattern

Chasing fees drains your staff and strains the relationships you most want to keep.

Reminders go out late, or not at all. Your bursar spends the first weeks of term on phone calls nobody enjoys making — or receiving. Some parents genuinely forget; some are waiting to be asked properly; and some fees simply slip through, term after term, with the school quietly absorbing the loss.

It isn't a staff problem. Your bursary team is doing this work by hand because no one has ever built them a system.

Well-run schools don't chase harder. They follow up consistently, respectfully, and on time — every family, every term.

The system

Professional fee follow-up, working through the WhatsApp your parents already use.

Timely, respectful payment reminders — sent automatically through WhatsApp, SMS, or email, in your school's voice and on your school's schedule. Your bursary team sees everything, approves what goes out, and steps in only where a human touch is needed.

  • Reminders parents don't resent — courteous, well-timed, and consistent for every family
  • A live view of who has paid, who is pending, and who needs a personal call
  • Nothing sent without your team's oversight — the bursar stays in control
  • Fits your existing fee schedule and records; no new software for staff to learn
How it works, honestly: the system drafts and schedules the messages; your staff review and approve them. It removes the repetition, not the human judgement. Your data stays yours — see data & privacy below.

Data & privacy

Your school's data is handled the way you'd want your child's data handled.

Handled in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act (2023). The full one-page data handling summary is available before any agreement — just ask.

The school remains the owner of all its data, and can export or delete everything on request.
Only the information needed to run the system is stored — fee schedules, balances, and contact details. Nothing more.
Student and parent data is never sold, shared with third parties, or used to train AI models.
Access is limited to your named staff and me; every message sent is logged and visible to your bursary.
